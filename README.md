# Claims Triage Copilot

**AI claims triage — FNOL intake, severity scoring, fraud flags and smart routing.** Part of the [Zion AI App Network](https://ziontechgroup.com/apps/network.html) (Batch 101 — Insurance & Risk AI) by [Zion Tech Group](https://ziontechgroup.com/).

## What it does
- **FNOL intake automation** — capture first notice of loss from email, voice and web forms; auto-extract claimant, policy, loss facts.
- **Severity & complexity scoring** — route fast-track vs. complex claims to the right adjuster queue in seconds.
- **Early fraud flags** — surface suspicious patterns at intake and hand off to [Insurance Fraud Sentinel](https://github.com/Zion-support/insurance-fraud-sentinel).
- **Cycle-time analytics** — measure touch time, leakage and SLA risk per claim.
